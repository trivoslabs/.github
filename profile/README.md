# Trivos Labs
**Engineering Machines Intelligence · هندسة ذكاء الآلات**

Trivos Labs is a Dubai-based AI development and consultancy company, licensed in Dubai Silicon Oasis since 2025, working with organizations across the Middle East and North Africa. Our mission: "To empower organizations by transforming complex challenges into growth and efficiency with innovative, value-oriented AI solutions."

## What we do
- SME Services — retail, clinics and small businesses.
- Enterprise Services — real estate, education and other established organizations.
- Government Services — government bodies and authorities.

Our work spans AI solutions, the data and systems work that makes AI possible, and research and consultancy.

## What we publish here
This organization's public repositories are the lab's open output: small, focused, dependency-light packages for the UAE market, and engineering notes on how we set things up.

- [uae-occasions](https://github.com/trivoslabs/uae-occasions) — UAE public holidays, national observances and international days, bilingual (EN/AR), with Hijri dates via the built-in Umm al-Qura calendar. Zero dependencies.
